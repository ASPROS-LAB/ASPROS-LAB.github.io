## About ASPROS

The ASPROS (Autonomous SPacecraft and RObotic Systems) Laboratory is part of the Department of Astronomy / Satellite Systems at Yonsei University, founded and led by Dr. Hancheol Cho. ASPROS conducts research and technology development in various fields of space engineering, including satellite orbit/attitude determination and control, satellite formation flying, on-orbit servicing, space debris removal, space-based space surveillance, and Mars EDL (Entry, Descent, and Landing) studies.

In collaboration with Professor Sang-Young Park’s ACL (Astrodynamics Control Laboratory) in the same department, ASPROS members design, develop, manufacture, test, launch, and operate low-cost CubeSats, thereby pursuing a balance between theory and practice while leading space engineering in Korea. Furthermore, ASPROS strives for groundbreaking advancements and demonstrations of new technologies through diverse international collaborations with countries such as the United States, Canada, and Japan.
